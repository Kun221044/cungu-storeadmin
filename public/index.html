Quote requests

Requests customers send from My list on the two stores. Check stock, change quantities if needed, reply to the customer, then Accept once they confirm: the items come off that store's stock as a sale with the quote number as the reference.

2027 purchasing plan

Imported products are stocked: deliveries in the delivery months set under Setup, ordered a month earlier, each covering use to the next delivery plus safety stock, run down so nothing is carried into 2028. Local products are bought in the month of use. Driver use comes from the 2027 budget (8 Gauteng drivers); catalogue items use their projected yearly sales spread January to October. Edit use per month in each product.

Import orders by supplier

Cape Town engine programme (extra)

The 4 Cape Town client drivers use half the Gauteng engine-part quantities (same engine timing), plus 20 walk-in top-end kits (2 a month, January to October). Order with the SA imports and send down with the advisory trips.

Store feeds

How the two stores connect. Each store reads its live feed from this server: /api/feed/sa.json for the SA store and /api/feed/ke.json for the Kenya store (CSV versions at .csv). Prices, stock and photos update on the stores within a minute of a change here. Photos are served from /api/img/…. When a store makes a sale it reports it to /api/stores/sa/movements or /api/stores/ke/movements with its store key, and stock comes off automatically. Quote requests from My list on the stores arrive under Quotes.

Put the live shop on a store (one line)

Add this line to the store page's HTML. It shows the full catalogue - categories, search, sort, photos, prices, stock - with My list and Request a quote, all live from this Store Manager. Put <div id="cungu-shop"></div> where it should appear, and list the old product and list sections in data-replace so they are hidden.

Feed format (for the store developer)

JSON object with store, currency, generatedAt (ISO time) and products. Each product: sku, name, category, description, imageUrl (main photo), images (all photos, main first; space separated in the CSV), options (sizes etc., comma separated), unit, price (incl. VAT, store currency), priceExVat, vatRate, stock (units on hand at that store), availability (in_stock, low_stock or on_order), leadTimeDays, updatedAt. The CSV feed has the same columns. Only active products ticked for that store are included.

Kenya prices are worked out as: SA retail excl. SA VAT × (1 + uplift) × exchange rate × (1 + Kenya VAT), rounded up. A KES price typed on a product overrides this. The 25% uplift is an estimate: set it to your real freight and duty cost.
